Colour vision
Protanopia, deuteranopia, and tritanopia simulations are regression-tested. Error/success and add/delete may converge under some simulations, so the runtime also uses diagnostic letters, undercurls, gutter signs, and distinct diff surfaces.

          
Font neutral
These renders prefer AtkynsonMono Nerd Font Mono, Medium, 15 pt when installed, then fall back to the platform monospace. The font is not bundled or required.

          
Provenance
Physical ink and CC0 Paleto palettes informed relationships, never exact screen values. Read third-party references and the full contrast report.

# Third-party notices

Summing depends on MonoGame Framework DesktopGL and transitive NuGet packages; their package metadata contains the authoritative licenses and notices. No third-party gameplay source or art asset is copied into the runtime.

The named games and art references in design documents are high-level design vocabulary only. No proprietary sprite, pose sheet, palette, character, stage, faction, terminology, text, sound, or code from those references is distributed here.

Ozzbit Games' Template Free character sheets are included as the sole player sprite family. The repository owner identified themself as their creator and explicitly authorized their use here on 2026-09-07; the bundled license, readme, archive hash, and authorization record are retained with the assets. Other downloaded art packs remain ignored local ZIP archives and are not runtime assets or generative inputs. See `third_party/art/catalog.json` and `docs/art/SPRITE-GENERATION.md`.

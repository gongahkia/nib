# Tone and world lock

## Emotional contract

Summing is lonely, melancholic, sacred, grotesque, beautiful, and mysterious. There is no humour: no jokes, irony, comic relief, whimsical naming, or knowing commentary. Silence is intentional in the first slice.

Traversal through an empty space should remain tense and technically engaging. The landscape is not filler between authored events. An exposed machine rib, a cliff shrine, a fossil conduit, or an eroded ownership mark must imply human consequence without stopping play to explain itself.

The visual hierarchy is silhouette first, material second, detail last. A disciplined dark mineral palette carries the landscape; globally consistent pale-gold and blood-red accents mark the player, interaction, danger, and recovered knowledge. Ruins must look embedded in geology rather than placed on top as conventional platform rooms.

## The world: the Lorn Reach

The slice crosses the Lorn Reach, an open cliff country left when the inland atmosphere thinned and the old reservoir seas escaped upward as vapour. Layered mesas expose settlements from epochs that never knew one another. Some walls are architecture; some were once machines; later peoples treated both as sacred geology.

Generated histories use original vocabulary assembled from seed-bound language fragments. Their cultures can disagree about the same remains:

- the **Votive Calculi** treated computation as a debt recorded in fired mineral tablets;
- the **Ash Meridian** marked safe migrations with vertical red cuts in cliff faces;
- the **Choir of Measures** enclosed fossil pressure organs and interpreted their weathering as law;
- the **Pale Convenant** is a later scholarly label, not a stable universal truth.

These are tone examples, not fixed canon. Each seed generates new names, practices, migrations, conflicts, relations, monuments, and collapse causes. Factions in this slice are dead or absent. They appear only through provenance: masonry patterns, pigments, altered material, records, relics, and overlaid territorial marks.

## Archaeology and motive

The player is a custom, deliberately anonymous climber. No authored identity is imposed. The immediate objective is to reach the exposed summit relay. Optional relics and records add fragments to a permanent cross-world Archive, even if the run ends. The Archive confers no power. A new world carries a new history; accumulated knowledge therefore becomes a cabinet of mutually incompatible dead worlds.

## Locked exclusions

This is not a cave descent, mining game, Metroidvania, combat showcase, momentum racer, or clone of any reference. The ascent must include lateral and occasional downward choices. Every movement action exists at the start. Progression is mastery, and the terrain is both route and adversary.

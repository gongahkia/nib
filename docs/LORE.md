# Lore contract

Palimpsest Run takes place in the Fold, a megacity repeatedly built over itself. New foundations pass through old homes, transit tubes become aqueducts, and obsolete civic machines keep serving purposes nobody remembers. The active layer calls the lower strata “underwriting,” as though architecture were a document.

The unnamed runner is consistent between runs but has no declared profession or destiny. They must keep moving. Scattered communities live around functioning pumps, heat exchangers, and artificial caretakers. During a run they flee, hide, wave warnings, secure shutters, or help one another without stopping the player.

Fragments are material evidence: maintenance tags, household inventories, machine dreams recorded as fault logs, children’s route marks, and contradictory evacuation notices. A collected fragment enters the local archive permanently and remains present in later runs for score. Fragments suggest that entire districts were deliberately buried, but do not settle who built the hand or why it pursues runners.

The hand is called nothing in-world. It is architecture-sized, runs on articulated fingers, loads surfaces with real weight, and damages the same city the runner crosses. Its origin stays unresolved in this slice.

There is no permanent increase in power. Persistence records lore, run history, scores, and cosmetic scarf colours only.
